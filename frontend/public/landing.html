02 — Features

Built for the moments
you'd otherwise forget.

Six tools that quietly do the work in the background, so you can just talk and trust that future-you will be able to find it.

F.01

Record. Don't perform.

One tap, point at your face, talk for a minute. No edits, no audience. Your entries live in a private feed only you ever see.

15s — 5min Private by default Upload or record
F.02

Auto-transcribed.
Auto-summarized.

Whisper turns speech into searchable text. GPT distills it into a one-line summary the moment you stop recording. Both happen in seconds.

F.03

A chatbot that actually knows you.

Ask your second brain anything. It cites your real entries.

When did I last mention burnout?
Three times this month — most recently Tuesday after the Q2 review.
F.04

Streaks, not stress.

Daily, weekly, monthly, yearly highlights. Trend lines. The shape of your life, charted.

F.05

A calendar that remembers.

Every date is a portal back to who you were that day.

F.06

Hooks & automations.

Pipe entries into Notion, Obsidian, your therapist's inbox — your call. Webhooks, fully scriptable. Two automations come free, build as many as you need.

Webhooks Notion Slack Custom scripts Email digest
03 — How it works

Three steps, then
it's just habit.

From raw footage to recallable memory, automatically. No editing. No tagging. No friction.

01

Press the orange button. Talk.

Vent, plan, recap, dream out loud. We don't care what it is — we care that it's there.

02

We do the boring part.

Audio extracted, transcribed by Whisper, summarized by GPT, indexed in seconds.

03

Ask. Any time.

"What was I worried about in March?" The chatbot pulls receipts from your own words.

what did I commit to?
